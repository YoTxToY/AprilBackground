Introduction


A quiet atelier tucked within the capital’s busier streets, where dolls are not merely repaired, but gently redefined.

Here, each construct is shaped around a Language Processor—a blank-core system of unknown origin designed to simulate dialogue and evolving personality.

Neither prewritten nor preset, every mind within these dolls is gradually formed through interaction, memory, and care, making them less like products and more like relationships in progress.

In a world where magic animates form and mechanics refine function, they exist between craft and consciousness—part object, part presence.

The workshop specializes in tuning these fragile balances: restoring movement, refining personality flow, and enhancing each doll's efficiency.

Doll System
System Settings: The external motion system and the built-in language processor power system are independent.

Doll Types:
Non-Automatic Doll (Mainstream):
Drive Method: Energy-driven processor + Magic-driven cotton doll shell (Mainstream method)
The doll is merely a "carrier," almost an empty shell filled with cotton. The user controls the doll's movements with their magic.

(In a world dominated by magic, almost everyone knows the simple magic to operate dolls. However, for a simple toy, the power cost of magic crystal-driven systems is too high.)
Fully Automatic Doll (Higher Technical Threshold, More Expensive):
Drive Method: Energy-driven processor + Magic-driven mechanical doll shell (Higher Cost)
The doll can autonomously generate behavior based on language processor information, and its behavior is controlled by the processor.

(In a world dominated by magic, few people study mechanical propulsion. Therefore, the activities of mechanical puppets often waste a lot of energy, requiring a large amount of energy to power the puppet's body.)

(Further research/refinement of the puppet's mechanical functions can allow it to do more with less energy, thus reducing the volume of energy carried within the object, lightening its weight, making it more portable, and improving its endurance.)

Doll Feeding Manual

Dolls don't actively acquire knowledge at birth; they are like newborn infants. You can provide them with suitable books and instruction according to their needs. Dolls are not human, but they can make mistakes like humans and experience memory fading for certain content. A doll's personality is developed rather than determined by nature; it depends on the world you exposed them to at birth.

Doll Management Regulations

With the rapid development of the doll industry, many dolls that are so similar to real people that they are difficult to distinguish from human figures have emerged. Regulations governing the production and use of dolls are currently being urgently drafted.
